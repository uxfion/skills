# Reports

Every page that shows the work is held to one standard, whoever reads it: a report the user reads, the page the user presents at a group meeting, a technical report for a hospital, a talk, a Word file. The user, 2026-11-14: 「都是呈现。目标是把事情/问题讲清楚梳理清楚展示好」; 「不要去刻意把报告或者组会或者对外演讲给分开来」. This replaces the split into an internal report and an outward document of 2026-11-10. The standard is the default. Where a page's use (spoken over at a meeting, or sent to be read alone) changes something, the rule says so where it stands: how much text and how a figure is explained, how much of the history of the work is shown, which parts of the frame a page has, the medium. A manuscript follows the papers reference of this skill, with *What the page claims*, *One voice* and *Words* from this one; its structure and its figures are the manuscript's own.

The standard was drawn from the three pages the user named as satisfying on 2026-11-14 (a group-meeting page from each of two projects and a technical report for a hospital) and from the corrections that led to them.

## The page answers its reader's questions

Before writing, design the structure and the line of argument from the questions this page answers for its reader: 「你要自己整理一下，设计一下结构，如何合理表述，然后再动手写」. The questions are the reader's own. For a group meeting they are what the last meetings asked, each answered: 「我们的汇报要有针对性，针对某个问题去讨论，而不是泛泛而谈」; 「最近两次开会的那几个点，报告里都有回应或者展示吗？」. For a hospital they were why the work is needed, what it does and how, its key techniques, its effect and what follows. Where nobody asked, the sections follow the topics of the work: 「按照问题或者我们的干的事情主题来」. When the request does not say whom the page is for and how it will be used (presented at a meeting, sent to be read alone, printed), ask in one line before starting; most requests say it (组会, 发给医院, a Word file).

An earlier report is a source of facts, never the outline, as the common block says of any old document: after the user pointed at an earlier report and the agent copied its shape, 「也不是一定要一样，你现在这个报告条理太不清晰了」. A case worth knowing: once, before a report was written, its outline was put in the chat in a few lines, and the user corrected its starting point there, before a page existed.

A page stands alone: 「全新的一次汇报，历史的那些东西我不会去看，只看我们现在做的这一份报告。所以，你要讲得全面一点」. It states the task and shows its input (the user, of a page that opened on results: 「一开始那个视频也要展现进去，因为我现在不清楚任务的输入是什么」), says what was done and on what data and how the data was selected, explains its terms and its key techniques until a reader meeting them for the first time follows, and carries its references: 「还有报告里数据集也要提到，用了什么数据。都要清晰的展现出你做的工作。不然我都不知道你做了什么，有什么结果。」

Before a group meeting the results are settled first, and the time left goes to the page; this is how both satisfying meeting pages were made: 「开组会前实验结果基本定型了，然后就集中注意力去优化报告」. What is not finished by then is shown for its direction: 「有些实验没做完，但是有方向性的效果或优化也可以展示，并不是一定要全部跑完才可以汇报。方案和思路也可以汇报，但最好是有主图辅助」.

## The first screen shows the whole

Before the first section the reader sees what the work is and where it stands: the task in one sentence, and the result at once. The result is one worked case from the input to the output and its reference, the input, the method and the output side by side, or the conclusion of the whole as one figure; three or four key numbers may stand beside it, each with a line saying what it measures. Minutes before a group meeting the chosen result once sat far down a report: 「既然能选出来，你就在报告里展现出来，而且要放在前面。」 A page answering questions someone put to it (a meeting's) follows with its answer table: one row per question, with where the question came from (the meeting and its date), the answer in one sentence, its section, and its state (done, partly done, on hold) as a coloured chip.

## Each section is carried by its main figure

The satisfying pages show their results one way: 「他其实每个问题，或者每个主题都是有一张主图的。非常清晰。」 A section goes:

1. The heading states the section's conclusion, with its number where it has one, or asks its question with the conclusion directly below.
2. The answer, in a sentence or two.
3. The main figure: the section can be understood from it without the text.
4. Under it, how to read it (the panels, the colours, the marks) and what it shows (the conclusion, and how far it reaches). On a page spoken over at a meeting these are two short labelled lines, 「怎么看：」 and 「说明什么：」; in a document read alone, the figure's caption says both. The user, of a figure without them: 「有图，但是我不知道那个图是什么意思啊。你要跟我讲清楚」.
5. The supporting figures and tables, numbered. Detail the argument does not need goes into a fold or the appendix.
6. What the group meeting is to settle, where the page is presented at one, closes the section.

A main figure carries its conclusion as its title. It puts the images that show the effect and the numbers that measure it into one figure: 「二、三、四都要有效果图支撑，不能就说一个结论，那肯定不行」. One colour stands for one group all through it; a reference is marked as a reference and is not ranked; each panel says which direction is better. States are drawn in: solid for what is done or evaluated, dashed for what is under way or planned. A method or a route drawn as a main figure shows each step by a real image where the step has one, with the steps the section is about set off by a tint: 「流程图看得不是很直观，应该要把它弄成类似主图的形式」.

## The frame

These are the parts of the satisfying pages, in their order; a page has them by default, each where its second column says. Where you leave one out, have a reason you could give.

| Part | Where | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| Navigation | A long page | A bar fixed at the top with one short name per section |
| Eyebrow (眉题), title | Every page | Topic · date · what the page is (组会汇报, 技术报告); the task or the question in one sentence |
| Scope | Every page | The data, and whether it also served to choose the method; the model; how the comparison was run; whose readings the judgements are. In a line or on the key-number cards; on a meeting page also how the page reads ("each section gives its answer first, then the main figure and the numbers") |
| First screen | Every page | The result at once (*The first screen shows the whole*) |
| Answer table | A page answering questions someone put to it, as a meeting's | One row per question (*The first screen shows the whole*) |
| One section per question | Every page | A small label above the heading names it (§1 · Q1, or the topic); then as *Each section is carried by its main figure* says |
| Next step | Every page | What comes next, with a figure of the route |
| Appendix | More than the argument needs | The other tables and cases; the longer account, as a second page in the same folder, where one is wanted |
| References, files | References where cited; files where the reader can use them | The literature cited; the scripts behind each figure and table, the experiment folders, the git revision |

## What the page claims

- **A claim is worded no more strongly than its evidence, and its boundary is said once, short and neutral, where it bears:** the scope line for the page as a whole, a clause in what a figure shows ("one seed, n = 20: the direction only"), a note under a table, and the limits of the work, checked against the scope in `docs/goal.md` and this work's own, each beside the step that addresses it. A conclusion carries its state; a starting vocabulary, to extend where a case needs another word: 已验证 / 部分验证 / 未实现 / 候选. Verdict words are neutral (良好 / 待复核). Every true fact stays on the page, placed and worded as part of the work; a boundary repeated through a page reads as the page taking itself apart: 「你这不是自己否定自己吗？」; 「就感觉你在拆自己的台一样」.
- **What was tried is shown as far as it answers the reader's questions.** At a meeting that is every direction tried since the last one, the failed ones with real images, the reason and the next step (「记得在报告里也要记录不同方向的尝试及效果图，这样在汇报的时候才有说服力。」), and each decision with the question it answered and the options weighed (「把核心问题（需要考虑的点）列出来，还有方案，而不是就把决策直接按点列出来。这是组会报告」); where the newest results fall short, the effects shown at earlier meetings are shown again (「我说了很多次，效果效果效果！即使你后面开发模型效果不好，那至少也要把上次汇报那些大致有效果的展示放进来，可以重复的」). In a document sent to be read alone it is the settled work, its design choices each with the reason, and the history of attempts stays in the project's records: 「要是一份比较完善的报告，是一个整体，一份技术报告，而不是堆积的实验和尝试，是对外部展示的一份整体技术报告。」
- **Results are shown generously, with good examples:** 「结果展现要丰富一点，图多一点没事，就是要被人看到效果」, 「例子挑选比较好的」. A picked case is labelled as picked; where the page speaks of the average, randomly drawn cases stand beside the picked ones: 「你再选几例比较好的补充上去吧，随机的6例也保留了。」 Picked cases show how a method works, not how often (the experiments reference of this skill, *What a result may claim*). Among the cases shown, each kind of failure the reader should know of has its case.
- **A question for the user goes to the user in the chat,** with the page as its evidence, the way the common block describes. Lists of pending decisions placed at the end of reports, across projects, went largely unanswered.

## One voice

The page presents the work as the user's: 「技术报告中不该出现我和你分离的这种情况，展现人都是我」. Whoever did a part (a script, the agent, the user) is told as one author: 「这三方都是要统一成一个整体去叙述的」. The working process stays out of the argument: how the agent checked its work, a working principle, an instruction of the user's, who decided what. A step that is truly part of the method stays, told as the method's step. One case: a section that told how the agent had reviewed frames by eye drew 「看图审查只是我在让你实现的过程中讲到的东西，这不应该呈现在技术报告中吧」; the figure stayed, and the section was retold as the method's quality control, describing what the method checks. A judgement made by looking is said as what it is (目视检查，未经医生复核), not as an expert's reading. Experiment codes, decision and to-do ids (D47, B7), version labels and file paths stand in the files part of the appendix, where the reader can use them.

## Words

- **Plain words, and every term of the project's field explained where it first appears,** all through the page: 「另外qc是什么，我都不清楚，解释这个名词，全程」; 「所有的报告都要求白底，学术导向，说人话」. The user knows machine learning and is not a specialist of the project's field, so machine-learning basics need no explaining and the field's terms do. Which field that is belongs to the project's own text; a reader from outside may need more.
- **How much text follows the page's use.** A page the user speaks over at a meeting has few words: 「汇报要抓重点，不能长篇大论的，用简洁，清晰的话表达出意思，因为汇报的时候不太会看大段的文字」; 「展示的 HTML 字可以稍微少一点，但是关键的内容我们要讲清楚」. A document sent to be read alone argues in full, its key techniques explained until they are easy to follow: 「文字部分，我感觉可以稍微丰富一点，论述得全面一点。特别是那些技术关键点，要讲清楚、理清楚，让人一读就很容易读懂」 (2026-11-04, of a technical report for a hospital). Either way the words argue in connected sentences; tables and short labelled lines carry comparisons and lists. A page of bullet fragments reads as machine output: 「我看到很多bullet point和标号，感觉让人不是很舒服，很像AI产出的，必要时可以加，没必要的话，稍微避免一点，在论述的时候。」
- **Length follows the content:** 「不要太长篇大论，也不要太简略，围绕着关键点，收放得当」; a page count mentioned on the way is a scale, as the common block says of any loosely given number (「17页就17页吧，页数没要求」).
- **A quoted English sentence** is copied as it is, with its Chinese on the line below.

## Results are seen at a glance

The user's standard: 「需要展现效果时，展现方式要更直观，让人一眼就能看出来，而不是放在不同的块上很难比较」. What must hold:

- **What is compared sits in one row.** For one case: input, result, reference, in that order. For methods: the same case and the same view, side by side. For many cases: one row per case, the same columns in every row.
- **One case, or one comparison group, fits one screen** (「而且一个案例尽量在一个HTML报告的滑动窗口里面。」). Use a full-width grid. Three layouts fail this and were each rejected: thumbnails (「缩略图我不可能再点进去的。」), one image per row so that a case takes several screens, and a single strip thousands of pixels wide. Secondary cases go into a fold, in the same layout.
- **Every image says what kind of thing it is:** observed data, a model's output, a candidate, a human-confirmed reference. The kinds keep separate labels, and an interactive element says whether it shows real data or a prediction.
- **The reference is shown, once per group.** A result without its reference cannot be judged (「另外展示的视频也要有一列是分割的标签叠加图，这样才有GT的参考」), and a reference drawn over every image hides the images (「也不是所有图都要上mask，你不觉得这样影响数据吗，增加分割图就可以，而不是叠加在所有图上」). So: one panel per group with the reference overlaid on the input, the other panels clean. A video carries a reference pane beside it.
- **The cases shown cover every category** the work covers: 「还需要在最后加入大量案例图，每种类型都要有。」
- **Where cases are judged one by one** (is this output usable?), each case image carries its verdict, coloured green (usable), amber (doubtful) or red (not usable), and the group's conclusion stands in its last cell.
- **A video is shown as mp4,** which plays in the page and on the user's machine (「将其也一样转成mp4方便我查看」). Where the medium cannot play video or turn a 3D model (Word, PDF), show more stills: several frames, several views, several cuts.

## Figures

- **Every figure is looked at before its caption is written.** Open each figure you place, sample a few frames of each video, and write down what you see before saying what the figure shows or giving a verdict: 「都要抽样几个做视觉评估的，不然都不知道对不对」.
- **Text inside a figure is English by default** (the common block's rule): its script takes the language option, and a page gets Chinese figures when the user names Chinese for it. Inside means drawn into the image: axes, legends, annotations, labels. The caption and the text around the figure are in the page's language. More specific than the block: on a group-meeting page in Chinese, a schematic (a method overview, a technical route, a data flow) is drawn in Chinese and a plot of data in English, unless the user says otherwise; the user named Chinese for these at two meetings running (2026-11-12; 2026-11-13: 「另外示意图都用用中文」).
- **A figure is drawn at its final size and print resolution by the script that draws it,** and stays as drawn: enlarging or resampling it later in the document tool is the repair the user objected to (「你这些图绘制的时候不就应该是输出600dpi的png了吗？为什么在操作word这边。」). The measure is that it looks clear on the page, not that nothing is lost. When every figure of one Word report was drawn at 600 dpi the file grew very large, and the user said 「也不是说完全无损，只要看上去比较清晰就可以了」 and, for that report, 「就留300dpi的就行了」. That number is the user's for one printed report: it gives the scale, and the measure decides.
- **Raster figures on a page or in a Word file are saved as PNG:** 「另外，我们存图的时候都用PNG吧，因为JPG是被压缩过的」. The figure format of a manuscript is the manuscript's own matter.
- **A flow chart runs left to right:** 「流程图尽量不要竖着放，竖着放我一页不可能放得下，而且要滑页，根本就看不全，要横着放」. The user's test for any schematic or flow chart is that the figure alone tells the logic: 「看到图就能知道背后的逻辑和原理」.

## Comparison tables

The rule for a table that compares methods or runs by numbers is in the common block, and holds in the chat as in a file. This section is its form.

- The header of each numeric column carries its direction: ↑ higher is better, ↓ lower is better.
- Ranks are per column, among the ranked rows: ¹ is the best in that column's direction, and the cell ranked ¹ is also bold.
- A reference row (the raw input, an upper bound) says in its first cell that it is a reference, and carries neither rank nor bold.
- On a page, a table is drawn as in a paper (「表格，要按照论文里的风格来」): the methods carry the names their papers use, and ours stand apart from the compared ones. The user, of a page where they did not: 「哪些是我们的方法，哪些是对比方法，我看不太出来」.

An invented example:

| Method | PSNR ↑ | LPIPS ↓ | Dice ↑ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Input (reference, not ranked) | 24.10 | 0.310 | 0.712 |
| Method A | 27.32³ | 0.214² | 0.846³ |
| Method B | 28.05² | 0.221³ | **0.871¹** |
| Ours | **28.91¹** | **0.187¹** | 0.866² |

What a result may claim, a result on a small sample included, is in the experiments reference of this skill.

## Text by hand, scripts for what is redone

The text and the page are written by hand. Scripts do only what may have to be redone: cutting and assembling figures, building tables, computing numbers. They sit beside the page, run under uv, and write their numbers to a file next to them. An HTML page itself is never the output of a script (a Word or PowerPoint file is built by a script from text written by hand and kept beside it): 「直接写html而不是用代码生成html，图表等你可以用代码运行得到。」 The user's reason is that the text is the writer's own analysis and synthesis, which does not repeat: 「因为是你的分析归纳总结，不可重复」.

Every number on a page can be followed to a script and its output file, and the page says which.

## Making the page

The user's standing choices for an HTML page:

- **Tailwind and Mermaid from a CDN:** 「报告使用 CDN 上的 Tailwind 进行布局和样式设置，并使用 CDN 上的 Mermaid 来绘制图表，尤其适用于能够可靠地展现结构图/流程图/序列图的情况。」 Layout and style come from Tailwind (`https://cdn.tailwindcss.com`), with a few semantic classes of your own defined by `@apply` inside `<style type="text/tailwindcss">`, as in the example below.
- **Set no font family of your own** (no `font-family`, no `font:` shorthand); set sizes and line heights only. A named font that was missing on the user's machine fell back to one that is hard to read, and the user's answer was 「也不用刻意设置，就是它默认是什么字体」.

### The look

The user asked that the look be described in words, so that it need not be pointed out again: 「不是把样式提取出来，而是口述出来，用人类的语言描述出来。」 One thing in it is the user's rule: the background is white, always (「一定是白色的，我就不喜欢黑色背景。」). The rest describes the pages the user named as the look they want; its numbers are measurements of those pages and give the scale, not targets.

It is a printable academic technical page, not a slide deck and not a product page. One restrained accent colour (a deep teal or steel blue) marks headings, rules and boxes; the rest is thin light-grey lines and space. The content is about 1150 to 1440 px wide, paragraphs at most about 1240 px, body text 16 to 17 px with line height 1.7 to 1.8; a main figure takes the full width. The label above each heading is small and in the accent colour; states are small coloured chips. Callout boxes, tinted with a coloured bar on the left, keep one meaning per colour: blue the answer, green how to read a figure, teal what is to be settled, amber a caution, red a warning. A grid image opens its full-size file on click, and a key region has a pair of enlarged crops beside it. Folds and controls may disappear in print; the conclusions and the figures remain.

### What the tools were seen to do

Dated hints; judge each against what the tools do now.

- **A semantic class that carries a Tailwind utility's name breaks the page** (seen 2026-09-24; seen again 2026-11-10). `.grid{@apply grid …}` is a circular `@apply`: the CDN compiler drops the whole style block, base styles included, and every image stacks vertically at full size, which the user met as 「现在报告完全不可读」. The same goes for `.flex`, `.table`, `.hidden`, `.block`, `.container`. Names that have worked: `.fig` for an image grid, `.cols2`, `.cols3` and so on for its columns. A search of the style block for a class that applies its own name finds the fault before the user does.
- **Mermaid rejects some text unless it is quoted** (seen 2026-09-24). Node text with punctuation needs double quotes (`A["…"]`), a dotted edge's label is written `-.->|"…"|`, and a semicolon inside a sequence message breaks the parse. The user has met a diagram's syntax error before the agent did; look at each rendered diagram.

## By medium

- **A page shown in a talk** has to work where it is shown, so it may carry its own stylesheet and local copies of its libraries instead of the CDN ones, and a long one may turn by screens; video and interaction go in where they show the result better: 「因为HTML会放到汇报演讲的时候去讲。」
- **Word and PowerPoint files** are built by a script kept in the report's folder, and checked in the application their readers will open them with. Nothing is installed for the check: installing another office suite to look at a Word file was refused (「windows上本来就有word啊」).
- **Questions for people outside** (a supervisor, a clinician, a data provider) are gathered as they come up and, before the meeting, put on one page with pictures: 「医生数据方面的问题最好做成一个HTML这样既可以展现问题，又有图像」. The form that has been used: an overview table (the question, whom it is for, what it decides), then for each question why it is asked, what each answer would change, the evidence images, and fixed answers to choose from. Which questions are worth an expert's time, and what is tried before asking, is in the experiments reference of this skill. Like everything that goes to people outside, the page goes through the user (the common block).

## The folder is a package

A report lives at `reports/<YYYYMMDD>/<task>/` (the layout reference of this skill has the whole table of places). The folder can be handed to someone as it is: 「我希望html是可以单独拎出来的」.

- Every image and video the page shows is copied into the report's own `figures/`, grouped by task or case, and linked relatively, as files and not as base64 inside the page. A README in `figures/` says which path or script each file came from.
- A version derived from a page (a Word file, a talk page, a page for another reader) lives in that report's folder, with its build script and a note on how it is built. The user turned down a separate folder for one: 「我觉得是不是也没必要新建一个文件夹，你就直接放在原来那个」.
- In git: the page, the scripts and small manifests. For images, videos and models made from patient data, the common block's guardrail on patient data holds.
- **A summary over a stretch of time** (before a group meeting) is a report like any other: its task folder is `reports/<the date it is written>/meeting-summary/`. It states the period it covers and uses its own copies of the figures. The dates of the work come from the records; a folder's date is not the date of its content.

## Checking and handing over

Before handing over: read the source through once, for tone as well as content, and open the figures you made.

Where a browser is already on the machine, headless included, open the HTML page in it and see that the layout holds: grids are grids, no table runs off the page, no diagram shows an error, no image is missing, a scripted control does what its label says. Fix what you find. Where there is none, say at hand-over that the page was not rendered. Install nothing for this, whatever the installer: 「卸载掉，没让你装。我自己会检查。」 Here this document is narrower than the common block's rule on installs. Whether the page reads well and looks good is the user's judgement. Both halves have their cases: the user was the first to see a broken page (every image stacked, a fallback font, a diagram's syntax error), and, before this was settled, the user objected when a browser tool was brought in and its system libraries installed to look at one (「你为什么要用playwright？」).

Then give the user the path; the page reaches them in the chat the way the common block has any long document reach them.

## After hand-over

- **A page the user has seen is a snapshot.** New findings go into a new report in a new task folder, and a reworked version is made as a new file while the old one stays (「你可以新建一份，原来的就不动了」). The exception is a page the user is still presenting from and wants extended: new material is appended as dated sections and what is there stays untouched (「现有报告现在就不要修改了，我觉得挺好的。有新的东西出来，你就追加进去」). A page for another reader (a hospital, a committee) is likewise written anew from the same results, in the same folder.
- **A page that a later one overtakes** stays as written and gets the dated line at its top that the common block gives any superseded document, naming the page that replaces it.
- **A statement on a page the user has seen that turns out never to have held** stays as written and is marked: a red box beside it says that it is wrong and links to the page that corrects it, a box at the top of the old page lists its corrections, and the new page says what it corrects. Everywhere else the false statement is taken out (the records reference of this skill, *Keeping records true*).
- **When the user asks for a change inside an old page,** the old content goes into a fold and the section opens with the date and the reason.
- **Each report gets a row in `reports/README.md`:** date, task, the question it answers, the core conclusion, the path. Where an index page stands over several reports (a day's `index.html`), it shows per task the question answered, the core finding, one or two typical images and a link into the report: 「你在index的时候也要放一两张典型的图，然后有个链接跳转过去。不然我怎么知道你具体哪个文件夹干了什么？」
