# Severity definition, written 2026-10-12 before any new-paper (set "new") grade was looked at

Severe (tier 1), any of:
- none: no crop on the figure, or the crop covers under about a quarter of it;
- split: the figure lies in two or more crops and none holds it whole;
- missing: figure content beyond titles and labels lies outside the crop: a panel, a plot, an image, or a text
  panel or box that is part of the figure (prompt, example text, question, UI text, case text);
- extra: the crop holds two or more lines of body text, a section heading, a table, or part of another figure.

Not severe (tier 2): only titles or headings over the figure or a panel (even two lines), axis labels and ticks,
legends, panel letters, sub-captions under panels, slivers of a header or frame, extra margin.

Applied by me to every grader "large", "none" and "split" verdict of the new set, and to any "small" whose note
names a panel, plot or text box. Every item whose tier differs from the grader's size is listed with its image for
the user. The report also gives the numbers with the grader's own sizes, unchanged (large/none/split = severe).
